    Manual reconstruction
    
      
    
        
    The shown track (automatic reconstruction, or the track saved before) stays displayed and its
          points are put in as if they were clicked. Correct them, or click new ones, then fit.
    
        
    Markers
    
        
      
          
    • Coloured squares (dashed lines): the clicked points; they are movable (drag them).
      • 
          
    • Black dots (dash-dotted line): the end points and vertex of the fitted track; they
            are not movable.
      • 
          
    • After Fit segments both versions of the track are visible: the clicked one (its
            lines turn white, semi-transparent) and the fitted one, which can differ (e.g. the fitted
            vertex moves). Drag the squares and fit again to update it.
      • 
        
    
        
    Clicking a point
    
        
      
          
    • Press Add segment (2-prong) / Add track (3-prong).
      • 
          
    • Click the point on one U/V/W projection: this sets its time (a vertical line marks it).
      • 
          
    • Click the same point on a second projection: the time of the first click is kept, the
            coordinate in the third projection is calculated from the geometry.
      • 
          
    • Drag a point (coloured square) to correct it; the old fitted line disappears until the next fit.
      • 
        
    
        
    Modes (Segment creation)
    
        
      
          
    • 2-prong: the first point is the common vertex, then one point at the end of each segment.
      • 
          
    • 3-prong: two points on each track (the second one at its end); the vertex is where the
            track lines cross (white square). At least two tracks are needed.
      • 
        
    
        
    Buttons
    
        
      
          
    • Fit segments: fits the track to the charge and shows it (black dots: ends and vertex).
      • 
          
    • Clear track: removes the points and the track, to start from scratch.
      • 
          
    • ▶ & Save: saves the event as shown (with the checked event types); saving it
            again replaces the earlier save. Switching manual reco off saves nothing and shows the
            automatic reconstruction again.
